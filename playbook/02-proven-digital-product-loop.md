# Proven digital product loop -- copy the demand, not the product

Sources, all self-reported and UNVERIFIED: Mr. AI CASH (YouTube) beBRtz_VSGU "How I'd Make Money with Claude AI in
2026 (If I Had to Start Over)" (2026-05-20, 233K views, the channel's top video) and UKxb_1irpDQ "The ONLY Way To Make
Money With AI Digital Products In 2026" (2026-07-23); earlier 0Z_Av3p5360 (a 7-day launch). The funnel shape overlaps
intake/pending/mollykeyser-digital-product-funnel.md and richardyu-digital-product-business.md. New here: the demand
research, variants behind a quiz router, and a small two-ad-set test.

## The method
1. FIND DEMAND THAT IS ALREADY PAYING. In the Meta Ad Library pick a high-income country, search digital-product words
   (ebook, guide, planner, template) and filter to ads that have run past the first few days -- a test usually runs
   about three days, so an ad still live after that is one the seller chose to keep paying for. Add TikTok Creative
   Center, and a fresh Instagram or TikTok account that engages with such ads until its feed fills with them. Log
   product, landing page and ad link in a sheet; collect about ten.
2. PICK WITH THREE CHECKS (his): the problem keeps recurring; the existing product can be improved; you keep seeing it
   advertised, so the seller is scaling it.
3. UPGRADE, DO NOT COPY. A new name and angle, a better layout (he builds it as HTML in A4 pages and exports a PDF),
   worksheets the buyer fills in.
4. VARIANTS. Split one product by age range, gender, goal or problem; each variant gets its own product page.
5. QUIZ ROUTER. A landing page whose first question sorts the visitor and sends each answer to the matching product
   page. His argument, UNVERIFIED: the buyer's question changes from "do I need this?" to "which one do I need?".
6. GUMROAD SETUP. Show the table of contents as an image on the product page; put a discount code in the description
   (his: $49.99 down to $37 with 25% off); add the ad pixel in Gumroad's third-party analytics settings so the ad
   platform sees purchases (Gumroad Help Center, "Third-party analytics": snippets run on the product page, after
   purchase, or both -- confirm the options in Settings before relying on them). He saw the pixel under-count when
   traffic passed through an outside quiz page first: TikTok reported 3 conversions, Gumroad showed 2.
7. TRAFFIC. Free: two to five short videos a day per platform (one scenario video, two quote or advice videos). Paid
   test: one campaign, two ad sets (interests against broad, or tier-1 against a wider set of high-income countries),
   three to five creatives in each, $2 to $15 a day per ad set, three days, then scale only the winner.

## His numbers (UNVERIFIED)
- Facebook test: $60 spend, 3 sales, $124 revenue; the ad set without interests spent with no sales.
- TikTok test: $26 spend, $59 revenue; the tier-1-only ad set had zero conversions.
Three sales per test is noise. A $50-$100 test can return a first signal; it does not show the product is profitable.

## Risk flags
- Both of his examples are weight-loss products. Health claims carry ad-policy and consumer-protection risk; do not
  copy the niche to copy the numbers.
- "Upgrade" must not become copying a competitor's text, design or brand.
- Scenario videos in which an AI person reports a result are testimonials. Label AI content and never present an AI
  persona's results as real.

## When the hold lifts: launch readiness
From Seijin Jung (@SeijinJung), co-founder of Enrich Labs, "Our launch hit 3.6m views on X in 3 days" (X Article,
2026-04-02). His figures (3.6M views, 9,000+ users, 500+ support tickets in three days) are UNVERIFIED and his product
is a SaaS agent, not a digital pack; the four lessons transfer:
1. X AS THE HUB. In his account the launch spread from X to newsletters, Threads, Instagram, YouTube and company Slack
   channels; the other platforms amplified what started there. The operator's X account is @ResiMindset (EARNING-MAP).
2. FILM IT YOURSELF, HONESTLY. No agency video: two founders talking to camera, a real demo and the raw screen
   recording, shot in about two hours. The response favoured authenticity over production value -- the same finding as
   youtube-growth playbook/01.
3. PREPARE FOR THE SPIKE WEEKS AHEAD. Raise rate limits with every API and integration partner, stress-test, and have a
   scaling plan ready before the day. For a Gumroad pack the equivalent is checking delivery, the discount code, the
   pixel and the free-kit download under load, and knowing Gumroad's own limits before a post can send traffic.
4. BE THE SUPPORT DESK FOR THE FIRST DAYS. Founders answered tickets personally, got on calls with early users and
   shipped several fixes a day. The first week's questions are the product roadmap.
