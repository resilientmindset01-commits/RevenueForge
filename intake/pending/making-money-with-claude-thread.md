## Skepticism / failure modes the thread flags (do not ignore)
- PowermanFriendship (30): LLMs are KNOWLEDGE ACCELERATORS. If you lack the aptitude
  and judgment to run a business, the LLM will not do it for you. "Go do a business"
  does not magically happen.
- Spend often eclipses revenue; plain automation has little standalone value unless
  it is your paid job (just_a_knowbody).
- AI SLOP GLUT: app stores are flooded with AI-built apps and almost nobody buys them
  (Its_Not_Important, citing a Pivot to AI video). Building for fun with $0 earned is
  the common outcome (SuperRon08: "not a single penny, but great fun").
- A five-word correction can eat a big chunk of a session's cost (echoes elsewhere) --
  cost discipline matters.
