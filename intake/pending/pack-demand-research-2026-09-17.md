## 3. What this says about the $29 Claude Code Power Pack
1. THE PRICE IS INSIDE THE RANGE. Rated Claude Code products on Gumroad sit at about $19-$49. $29 needs no change on
   this evidence. Bulk packs at $7-$15 set a low anchor in ads; do not compete on skill COUNT (8 against 52 or
   2,000 loses) -- compete on depth and on one named outcome.
2. THE DEVELOPER AUDIENCE IS SERVED FREE. Anthropic-backed free training and free newsletters are what developers see
   in ads. A paid pack aimed at "developers who use Claude Code" competes with free; one aimed at a specific job
   ("review AI-written code before it ships", "run an agent unattended without it wrecking the repo") does not.
3. ROLE FRAMING IS WHAT KEEPS ADS RUNNING. Every long-running paid skill ad names a role (consultants, interior
   designers, Amazon sellers, MSP owners), and the best-rated Gumroad items are named "OS for <role>". That supports
   playbook 02 steps 4-5: the same eight skills framed as variants for a solo builder, a team lead reviewing AI code,
   and a non-coder building with Claude Code, behind one quiz question.
4. HOOKS THAT RECUR: the pattern you rebuild every week; "you use Claude like a search bar"; random prompts give random
   results; the expert who is one person doing a team's job. The pack's own honest angle -- the failure modes a
   coding agent hits quietly -- fits the first and fourth without hype.
5. FREE SKILL PACKS AS LEAD MAGNETS ARE COMMON AND LONG-RUNNING. That matches the free 4-skill kit and the L=B plan:
   the free kit is the ask; the pack is the second step.
