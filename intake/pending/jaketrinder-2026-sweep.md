## Claims checked at source 2026-09-18
- "The goal of every video is viewer satisfaction, not retention... not click-through rate." PARTLY SUPPORTED.
  support.google.com/youtube/answer/16559650 names three buckets: "Appeal: Did people choose to watch the content or
  did they ignore or click 'not interested?'", "Engagement: Once viewers start watching, do they stick around?",
  "Satisfaction: Did viewers enjoy the video?" Satisfaction sits beside the other two, not above them.
- His composition of satisfaction (like/view, positive comments per view, comment sentiment, session duration): NOT
  published by YouTube. His model.
- vidIQ, TubeBuddy and 1of10 "are all done through YouTube's data API which means you can only access so much".
  THE LIMIT IS REAL: developers.google.com/youtube/v3/getting-started gives "a default quota allocation of 100
  search.list calls, 100 videos.insert calls, and 10,000 units per day combined for all other endpoints", with a form
  to request more. That those tools are confined to it, and that his own system is better for bypassing it, is
  UNVERIFIED and is a sales claim about competitors.
- The new-channel "sandbox": not an official concept; YouTube's public position is that channel size is not a ranking
  factor. The real effect is the cold-start data gap already in youtube-growth playbook/06.
- "Claude Code is a trending AI model": WRONG. code.claude.com/docs/en/overview: "Claude Code is an agentic coding
  tool that reads your codebase, edits files, runs commands, and integrates with your development tools." His separate
  point, that it is a trending video TOPIC, is fine.
- claude.ai/design, brand kit, design system handed to Claude Code: SUPPORTED,
  anthropic.com/news/claude-design-anthropic-labs ("Claude builds a design system for your team by reading your
  codebase and design files"), included with Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise.
- "Claude can't animate directly, so it uses Remotion": his 2026-06 workflow, not a product limit; the Claude Design
  announcement lists prototypes with video, shaders and 3D.
- "Set a cron job on Claude": the mechanism is Routines (cloud) or desktop scheduled tasks.
- NOT CHECKED, not carried: "YouTube videos are the number one cited source in LLM answers"; the 7-11-4 exposure rule
  attributed to a Google study; embedded-player watch time feeding organic recommendations; "top channels get 80% of a
  niche's attention"; every revenue, view and retention figure.
