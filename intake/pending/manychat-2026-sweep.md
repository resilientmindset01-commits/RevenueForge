## The rule that changes the design
VERIFIED at developers.facebook.com: a comment buys exactly ONE message -- "Only
one message can be sent to the commenter", within 7 days, and "Follow-up messages
can only be sent if the recipient responds, and must be sent within 24 hours of the
response."

If that one message is the download link, the conversation is over and the email
can never be asked for. The first message has to earn a TAP. Every funnel design
below depends on this.

## The decisions
### 1. Does this get built at all, and at what price
The free tier runs the triggers and can send a link, but EMAIL CAPTURE STARTS AT
THE PAID TIER. Stated allowances: Free is outgrown above 25 engaged people a month,
Essential 250, Pro 2,500 (quoted at 39 dollars a month plus 5 cents per extra
contact). So the free tier proves the flow; it cannot build the list.
DECISION: run the free pilot first (costs nothing, answers the PDF question), then
decide whether the list is worth a monthly fee at PrepBrix's current volume.

### 2. The PDF test, which blocks everything else
The file itself can now be sent inside a DM -- a PDF block in the flow builder, drag
and drop, no link and no website. But no size, page-count or format limit was stated
anywhere, and no list of which channels accept it. The two PrepBrix magnets are 1.3
MB and 523 KB.
ACTION, free and immediate: push both PDFs through a flow to a test account. If they
deliver, the operator has a distribution channel for assets that already exist. If
they do not, the flow sends a link instead and nothing else changes.

### 3. Is TikTok even available here
TikTok has no comment automation at all -- only an inbound DM keyword -- and the
automations are claimed to be business-account only and unavailable in the EU, UK
and Switzerland. India is not mentioned either way. Check before planning anything
TikTok-shaped.

### 4. The partner program, as a revenue line rather than a tool
The only contractual numbers in the batch: 30 percent commission, 40 percent after
30 sales, 50 percent after 200, paid on the referral's plan for 12 months. Plus 50
dollars per qualifying short video submitted under Challenges. This is a real
recurring-commission shape and it fits the operator's existing habit of writing up
what they build.
DECISION: GRADUATE / HOLD / NO FIT.
