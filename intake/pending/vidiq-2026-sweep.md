## The one dated fact that governs this whole category
The YouTube Partner Program entry bar doubles on 1 February 2027: 8,000 qualified
watch hours in 365 days, or 20 million qualified Shorts views in 90 days, on top of
1,000 subscribers. Today's 4,000-hour bar holds until 31 January 2027. Channels
already in the program are not affected. VERIFIED at
support.google.com/youtube/answer/12843009.

So any plan that routes money through YouTube ad revenue has a deadline attached,
and any plan that does not route through ad revenue is worth more than it looks.

## 26 methods, grouped by who pays
Written out in REVENUE-WAYS.md section 13. The four worth a decision:

### 1. The 500-subscriber tier arrives first and is being ignored
Memberships, Super Thanks, Super Chat and YouTube Shopping gate at 500 subscribers
plus 3,000 watch hours or 3 million Shorts views -- far below the ad-revenue bar,
and unchanged by the 2027 update. Reported first-90-day money for one creator was
43 dollars of supers plus 14 dollars of memberships, so this is not income yet; it
is the first rung that exists at all. The perks that cost nothing to produce are
early access, members-only streams and replays, custom emotes, end credits, and
answering member questions on camera.
DECISION NEEDED: set up the fan-funding tier on PrepBrix as the first money target,
or stay ad-revenue-only until 2027.

### 2. Amazon Influencer Program on-site reviews
The only method in the batch requiring no YouTube monetization, no audience and no
virality: 60 to 90 second product reviews posted into the product-page carousel,
earning commission on the referred cart, plus brands paying flat per-video fees for
reviews they liked. Claimed ramp 700 dollars in month one to 6,000 a month by month
eight (UNVERIFIED). Entry needs a public social account with no stated follower
minimum, judged on engagement.
DECISION NEEDED: this is a separate business from the education channel and would
compete for the same hours. GRADUATE, HOLD or NO FIT.

### 3. Search-intent affiliate as the main line, ads as the remainder
One operator claims roughly 80 percent of 300,000 dollars a month from affiliate
links against about 10 percent from ad revenue, on a portfolio of about 50 channels
where most are expected to fail. The scaled-down version they state is: one topic,
50 to 100 long-form videos answering buying questions, then your own course,
software or service.
Relevance here: PrepBrix already HAS the course-shaped product at the end of that
funnel, which is the part most creators lack. The affiliate middle is the piece
that does not fit an exam-prep audience with no purchase intent beyond books.

### 4. Run time, not upload count, clears the watch-hour bar
4,000 qualified hours is 24,000 full views of a 10-minute video, or 2,000 full views
of a 2-hour video. Long lean-back formats reach the threshold on a fraction of the
uploads. For a NEET channel that means full-length solved-paper sessions and live
problem marathons count differently from short explainers, and the 2027 deadline
makes that arithmetic urgent rather than academic.

## The reported faceless-channel spread, kept as evidence about niches
A 280,000-subscriber finance channel at about 5,000 dollars a month against a
300,000-subscriber channel at just over 1,000; a 166,000-subscriber
chat-compilation channel near 9,000 a month from 30 videos; a 60,000-subscriber
channel at 15,000; a 700,000-subscriber sleep-history channel at about 3,500 on
2.5-hour videos. All UNVERIFIED and all self-reported, but the spread repeats the
finding already in youtube-growth/playbook/07: subscriber count does not predict
the money, advertiser demand does. Education sits low on that ladder, which is why
the PrepBrix case was never ad revenue.
