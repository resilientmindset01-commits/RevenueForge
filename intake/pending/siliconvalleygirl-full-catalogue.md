## What the channel actually is
Three overlapping shows in one feed: (1) her own how-to videos on making and selling digital products, creator
income and AI tools; (2) founder and executive interviews (LinkedIn, Duolingo, Airtable, Anthropic, Replit,
Perplexity, Gamma, Granola, ElevenLabs); (3) career and jobs commentary built on third-party reports. Nearly every
video from 2024 on carries a sponsor, and in several the advice is shaped by the sponsor's product. Her own income
figures are self-reported and repeatedly bundle unrealized investment gains with earned revenue.

## The honest verdict
The interviews are mostly repackaged startup advice. The value sits in two places and both are already captured:
1. HER OWN OPERATING NUMBERS -- AdSense by channel and topic, video production costs, per-hire rates, email funnel
   metrics, a full product P&L, cost-per-follower on paid growth. That is the most useful material on the channel
   and it is now in RevenueForge/playbook/03-creator-rate-card.md and youtube-growth/sources/SiliconValleyGirl.md.
2. TWO METHODS WORTH COPYING -- the answer-engine visibility method (2NFQ4-6MzuU, folded into geo-distribution) and
   the AI-workflow patterns from Allie Miller and the Anthropic CPO episodes (folded into the Claude lanes).
Everything else is either already in the estate, generic, or dated.
