## The core model: sell an AI-POWERED SERVICE (not a digital product)
Take a skill you ALREADY have (sales, marketing, copywriting, design, finance -- whatever
your job pays you for) + use Claude to do ~90% of the work -> sell it to businesses as a
done-for-you SERVICE for $2.5K-$5K+/month (some deals $20K). You find + close the client and
own the relationship; Claude does the hard work; you manage (or subcontract the build).
Key reframe: "SELL BEFORE YOU BUILD." No upfront product, no testimonials needed -- a Loom
video + a diagram of your solution is enough to close. Businesses pay for ROI (you get them a
client worth $20K, they pay you $3K).

## Why he says other models fail (his framing)
Vibe-coded apps (can't monetize in a weekend; ~99% make <$100), faceless YouTube/IG (no moat,
demonetized, copyable), Etsy templates (copyable), AI dropshipping (needs ad spend + skill).
All require LEARNING new skills, COMPETING with experts, and UPFRONT work before a single
dollar. His model leans on skills you already have + fast cash.

## The repeatable method (the useful part)
1. FIND THE IDEA with Ikigai (love / good-at / world-needs / paid-for). He gives a FREE Claude
   SKILL that runs an Ikigai exercise -> a report: who you help, pricing, competitors,
   differentiation, a validation score /100. (This is itself the tactic: a free Claude skill
   as a LEAD MAGNET.)
2. BUILD A TARGETED LEAD LIST with a second free Claude SKILL: tell Claude your offer + ideal
   buyer SIGNALS (B2B, 11-200 employees, Series A/B funded, hiring content people, US tech),
   Claude finds + scores leads 1-10. "SNIPE, don't spray" -- target high buying-intent, not mass.
3. OUTREACH: LinkedIn DM (or Upwork / cold email) the fits; get them on a call. "Build a
   garden, don't chase" -- go where demand already is and solve a problem they have. He claims
   decision-makers respond even to beginners with no brand (UNVERIFIED client examples).
4. SELL ON THE CALL: understand the problem, present the solution (Loom + diagram), close. No
   proof required.
5. DELIVER: Claude does ~90%; you manage (client "Sandy" -> 20 min/day, UNVERIFIED).
6. PERSONAL BRAND raises reply rate: ~5 LinkedIn posts/day; even low engagement converts with
   the right offer. Graphics via Higgsfield + Claude (sponsor) in a brand style.
Weekend timeline (he admits it realistically takes ~1-2 weeks, not one weekend): Fri find idea
-> Sat build 50-lead list + outreach -> Sun ~40% accept, start messaging -> Mon-Thu calls ->
first client.
